Spot · Options Levels Tracker
—
Live candles from Twelve Data (your key, sent only to Twelve Data). Options levels, COT, macro and the model score come from the dashboard pipeline — the same data the Dashboard tab and the Telegram brief use.
What "Bullish/Bearish lean" means: a simple 3-point check against your entered/computed levels, not a signal or prediction — (1) spot vs gamma flip (only when dealer gamma actually flips sign): above = bullish point; (2) spot vs max pain: above = bullish point; (3) put/call ratio: under 1.0 = bullish point. The badge just counts how many of the available checks lean bullish. The model signal (Brief tab) is the one the Dashboard and Telegram use.

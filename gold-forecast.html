Forecasted levels vs current price

Right / shaded bands = intraday high–low  ·  left (blue, dashed) = expected open→close finish
Why the high and low bands don't add up to the range: each band is that side's own odds — the high tops its 90th band ~1 day in 10, the low breaks its 90th ~1 day in 10, but usually on different days. A single day rarely hits both extremes, so the real high-to-low range (the coloured strip) is narrower than High-band minus Low-band. Use the strip for the day's width; the bands for how far each side might reach.

Intraday revision — how much further can it run?

Enter the session high and low so far (not the current price — the signal is the range consumed, not where price sits). Levels are re-read from comparable historical days.
Read the median, then the tail. A busy session predicts a much bigger day in total (at the halfway point the busiest quintile finishes at ~1.49× the expected range vs ~0.83× for the quietest), but most of that is already behind you — so the typical further extension on a busy day is close to zero, because the extremes are usually already set. What does not shrink is the big row: a further large move stays just as likely. Mostly done, but not safe.
